# Gmail Farming & Anti-Robot Strategy

This skill provides a durable workflow for creating high-quality ("Badak") Gmail accounts that resist automated disablement and "robot" verification challenges.

## 🚀 Registration SOP (Anti-Robot)

To minimize the risk of immediate disablement or "Verify it's you" loops, follow this sequence:

### 1. Identity Isolation
- **Anti-Detect Browser:** Use browsers like AdsPower or Dolphin{anty}. Create one unique profile per account.
- **Network Hygiene:** Use Mobile Data only. Perform an **Airplane Mode ON $\rightarrow$ OFF** cycle between every 1-2 accounts to rotate the IP address.
- **Avoid WiFi:** Static IPs are easily blacklisted by Google.

### 2. Humanized Execution
- **Input Pacing:** Do not copy-paste data rapidly. Type names and birthdates with natural pauses.
- **Session Continuity:** Once the account is created, **do not log out immediately**. Stay in the session for 30-60 minutes.

### 3. Immediate Hardening (The Locking Phase)
Perform these steps while still in the initial registration session:
- **Recovery Email:** Immediately add a recovery email.
- **Cluster Strategy:** To prevent "Domino Bans," use a cluster approach: 1 recovery email per 5-10 accounts.
- **Recovery Provider:** Use non-Google providers (e.g., ProtonMail, Outlook) for recovery to isolate the account from Google ecosystem mass-bans.

### 4. Account Warming (Building Trust)
Simulate real human behavior to increase the account's "Trust Score":
- **Activity:** Open YouTube, watch a few videos, Like 1-2 videos, and Subscribe to 1-2 reputable channels.
- **Content:** Upload a short (5-10s), fresh-recorded private video to YouTube.
- **Interaction:** Perform a few random Google searches.

## ⚠️ Pitfalls & Troubleshooting
- **The "Robot" Loop:** If an account hits a verification loop immediately after creation, it is likely due to IP/Fingerprint reputation. Rotate IP and use a fresh browser profile.
- **The Domino Effect:** If one account is banned, avoid using the same recovery email for new batches.
- **Verification Walls:** If a phone verification is requested during login and the nokos (virtual number) is no longer accessible, the account is generally unrecoverable.

## ✅ Verification
An account is considered "Hardened" when:
1. Recovery email is set and verified.
2. Initial "warming" activities (YouTube/Search) are completed.
3. The account can be logged into on a different (but clean) profile without triggering a robot check.
