# Google Account Farming & Maintenance

This skill provides a durable workflow for creating and maintaining high-durability Google accounts to avoid "robot" detections.

## Pendaftaran (Registration) Workflow
1. **IP Hygiene**: Use Mobile Data. Perform **Airplane Mode ON -> OFF** between every 1-2 account creations.
2. **Device Fingerprinting**: Use an Anti-Detect Browser (e.g., AdsPower, Dolphin{anty}) with a unique profile per account.
3. **Registration Behavior**: Fill forms naturally; avoid rapid copy-pasting. Use high-quality SMS services for OTP.
4. **Immediate Hardening**: 
   - Set up a **Recovery Email** IMMEDIATELY upon first login.
   - Keep session active for 30-60 mins after creation.
   - **Warming Up**: Perform human actions (e.g., watch YouTube, search random terms).

## Maintenance & Recovery
- **Account Clusters**: Group accounts under a few recovery emails (e.g., 5-10 accounts per recovery email) to prevent "Domino" bans.
- **Verification Recovery**: Try logging in via native Android Gmail app on a fresh device/IP before browser recovery.

## Pitfalls
- **The Domino Effect**: Too many accounts per recovery email increases mass ban risk.
- **IP Blacklisting**: Repeated failures on one IP lead to hard blocks.
